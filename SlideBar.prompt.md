The header bar on a facilitation slide: what we are doing, and how long we have.

```jsx
<SlideBar title="let’s meet each other" duration="20 mins" background="var(--c-banana)" />
```

Titles are lowercase and human. The duration chip is always mustard and always flush right. Drop `duration` when the slide is not timeboxed; use `align="left"` when the title sits over a wide field rather than centred.
