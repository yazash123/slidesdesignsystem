The opening label of an explanatory slide — tells the room which part of the session they are in.

```jsx
<Eyebrow chip="var(--c-coral)">idea 3 — the chatbot</Eyebrow>
```

Inside a white panel, drop the chip and colour the text instead: `<Eyebrow color="var(--c-crimson)">in your own words:</Eyebrow>`.
