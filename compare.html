idea 3 — the chatbot

Asking it nicely vs asking it critically…

you

I’m excited about electric vehicles helping the environment. Are they a good idea?

chatbot

They’re a great choice! EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions, and the impacts of battery production can be managed to maximise the benefits over the car’s lifetime.

you

I’m concerned electric vehicles aren’t actually good for the environment. Are they?

chatbot

You’re right to be cautious. The impacts of battery production are significant, and mining for battery metals must be carefully managed. EVs are not a perfect solution.

same question + different tone = different answer
