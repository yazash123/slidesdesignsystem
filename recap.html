the drive home

The road behind us.

“AI” is a label, not one thing

A seventy-year-old term, used to describe very different things.

Three kinds of software

Rules-based — optimisation — machine learning.

idea 1 — the house

Drawing a line through data

Averages & optimisation

Using examples to learn a trend.

More data makes the line steadier.

Data must be relevant — there are lots of coincidences to be found.

idea 2 — the car

Stacking predictions in layers

Neural networks

We give the model all the clues; it finds their relative importance.

Tested against lots of examples and tuned, bit by bit.

No one can read the layers straightforwardly.

idea 3 — the chatbot

Predicting the next word

Large language models

Its main job: guess the next word in the sentence.

It mirrors your tone: questions influence the answers.

It pattern-matches, and sometimes makes things up.
